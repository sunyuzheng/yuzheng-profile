# Defining Idea And Intellectual Foundations

This file owns Yuzheng's durable public belief and the intellectual foundations beneath it. It deliberately separates five kinds of statement that used to be mixed together:

- a **defining belief** says what kind of work and life Yuzheng wants to stand for;
- an **intellectual foundation** explains what gives that belief substance without claiming a universal formula for success;
- a **practice principle** helps him pursue that belief without pretending to be a universal law;
- a **dated hypothesis** is a claim about a particular technological or business moment and must remain open to revision;
- a **product purpose or fact** belongs to the relevant product owner and must be judged by what the product actually delivers.

The first three live here. Dated hypotheses and public predictions live in [DATED_VIEWS.md](DATED_VIEWS.md) and the evidence ledger. Current product relationships live in [ECOSYSTEM.md](ECOSYSTEM.md).

## The Defining Idea

> **MAKE WHAT LASTS.**
>
> **做出你的代表作。**

This is the one defining idea behind Yuzheng's public work and Superlinear Academy:

> A person should have the chance to make work whose value outlives the moment of making and bears the mark of the maker's distinctive judgment and craft.

> 一个人应该有机会把自己的判断和本事，做成价值能够超越制作与首次发布的当下、也清楚承载自己独特判断与手艺的作品。

The English and Chinese lines enter the same belief from different sides. `MAKE WHAT LASTS` asks whether the work continues to matter beyond its making or first release. `做出你的代表作` asks whether it becomes distinctly yours: work that carries your judgment and craft, and that you are willing to put your name behind. If it keeps creating value, it may also return deeper capability, reputation, income, freedom, recognition, or a more ambitious next move. Those are desired returns, not part of the definition and not outcomes a slogan can promise.

`Lasts` does not mean immortal, unchanging, famous, laborless, or infinitely scalable. A lasting work can be a product, company, research program, method, system, course, book, film, craft, institution, performance tradition, or an entire body of work. It may be made by a team. Some work can travel without its maker; some remains alive because people continue to perform, maintain, teach, operate, or reinterpret it. The distinction is not whether future labor disappears. It is whether value, standards, capability, or influence extend beyond an isolated task or release. The phrase is an aspiration and a standard of judgment, not a promise that every worthy effort will endure or make its author successful.

Some work is valuable precisely because it answers a moment and then disappears. Care, repair, service, exploration, and necessary execution do not become lesser merely because they leave no durable asset. The belief asks a narrower, more personal question: **across a life, will some of your best judgment and craft take a form that keeps serving others and remains recognizably yours?**

The enemy is therefore not AI, speed, employment, or work done for other people. It is the default pattern of spending a life only completing tasks that reset tomorrow—becoming more efficient without developing authorship, mastery, accumulated choice, or work one is proud to be represented by.

## The Intellectual Foundations Beneath The Idea

A defining work often begins in uncertainty; exploration is one way the problem and the maker's conviction can emerge rather than a separate pillar. Beneath the work are two interpenetrating modes of knowing, conviction when action demands commitment, responsible authorship, and a world that remains free to refuse the work. These relationships interact rather than forming a production sequence; a maker cannot make something lasting merely by deciding to preserve it.

> **A defining work turns hard-won understanding and lived craft into responsible authorship—and remains answerable to reality, time, and the people it serves.**
>
> **代表作，是把逐渐挣来的理解与手艺，做成自己愿意长期负责、世界也愿意继续选择的作品。**

This is an account of what can give work unusual weight, not a success formula. Luck, resources, collaborators, institutions, power, distribution, and historical timing may all shape what survives and what receives recognition.

### Important work does not always begin with conviction

The clean story says that a person first sees the right answer, then persists until the world agrees. Much real work begins less neatly: with curiosity, a constraint, an assignment, a weak signal, an encounter with a material, or something made before its significance is understood. A person often has to enter the fog before knowing what deserves sustained attention.

Action creates evidence, but repetition alone does not create understanding. After an attempt, the maker has to ask what was expected, what happened, and which assumption or skill was missing. Conviction can initiate exploration; it can also be earned through making. This nonlinearity matters because a defining work is usually recognized more clearly in retrospect than it can be planned in advance.

### Explicit understanding: world models and the scientific method

A world model is an explicit account of causes, incentives, constraints, and likely consequences. The scientific method makes that account answerable: turn an explanation into a prediction, actively look for what would falsify it, and update when observation differs from expectation.

The point is not to collect correct forecasts or wait passively for “reality” to deliver a verdict. It is to design sharper contact with the world and become less able to hide from one's own error. Explicit understanding is especially valuable when it changes a consequential decision before complete evidence arrives. It remains limited where causality is inaccessible, feedback is delayed, or the world is too unstable for a useful model.

This is an emphasis, not a sealed compartment. Craft also contains causal expectations and working models; science tries to make more of them inspectable. Tacit perception can become explicit enough to test, and an explicit model can sink back into practiced judgment. The two modes continually correct and translate one another.

### Embodied craft: tacit knowledge that still answers for results

Craft is not merely the execution stage after a correct idea. It is a second way of knowing.

Science tries to make knowledge explicit, predictive, and falsifiable. Craft works inside dimensions of reality that cannot be compressed completely into rules but still have consequential differences between better and worse. An experienced builder, researcher, product leader, filmmaker, athlete, teacher, or carpenter can perceive distinctions and make trade-offs that a beginner cannot yet see or fully explain—and the result can still work better, stand longer, communicate more clearly, or fail less often.

This knowledge is earned through real problems, repetition, apprenticeship, comparison with excellent work, and attention to consequence. Tsunekazu Nishioka's temple carpentry is an important archetype in Yuzheng's own writing: knowing a tree so deeply that wood which had lived for two thousand years could be given another two thousand years of useful life. The point is not nostalgia or slowness. It is respect for material, complexity, and a standard that the finished work must actually meet.

The deeper the craft, the more complexity becomes visible; mastery expands the field of judgment rather than reducing it to a few tricks. Craft is also not a synonym for art. Many defining works combine verifiable understanding, result-answerable craft, and forms of expression whose worth cannot be exhausted by one outcome metric. AI can lower friction, expand iteration, and expose a learner to more examples. It does not make tacit judgment, responsibility, expression, or a standard of excellence automatic.

### Conviction: when understanding accepts the cost of action

Understanding can remain external: a persuasive idea, a borrowed conclusion, or something a person can repeat without being changed by it. A recurring sequence in Yuzheng's teaching is `understand → internalize → conviction → build`. Conviction is the bridge at the moment action requires a stake.

It is not stubbornness, contrarian performance, or confidence theater. It is a relationship among a belief, a person, and a cost: strong enough to support action before consensus arrives, and intellectually honest enough to change when better evidence appears. `Strong opinions, weakly held` is one discipline for maintaining that tension, not a personality test or a universal procedure. Conviction may precede a work, deepen through it, or disappear when the work teaches the maker something better.

### Responsible authorship: agency sustained through consequence

Authorship begins before a name is placed on finished work. It means participating in the definition of what should exist, whom it should serve, which standards matter, which compromises are acceptable, what evidence should change it, and who remains responsible for the consequences.

Duration alone is not a virtue, and authorship does not require one person to make everything. A team or institution can have authorship when its choices and standards remain coherent enough to be recognizable and when responsibility does not dissolve across contributors. Over time, a single work or a body of work may become difficult to separate from the maker's name. That is what `你的` asks for: not personal branding in place of substance, but agency, judgment, craft, and responsibility with enough continuity that the work genuinely represents its author.

### Reality tests; time reveals

Reality is not the third step of a method, and time is not a flawless final judge. Adoption can reflect distribution, lock-in, power, habit, or fashion; important work can be ignored for years. A scientific prediction, a product in use, a building that stands, and a film people remember offer different kinds of evidence and should not be collapsed into one metric.

They still do something the maker cannot do alone: prevent explanation from becoming self-certification. Users, constraints, consequences, evidence, criticism, maintenance, and changing conditions continue to test the work. Time reveals what people keep using, paying for, trusting, citing, watching, adapting, or remembering—and what decays despite a strong launch.

When valuable work continues to matter, value can move in both directions: the work keeps serving other people, while deeper capability, trust, income, freedom, recognition, and the right to attempt a more ambitious next work may return to its maker. Systems, institutions, stewardship, maintenance, and distribution may help create that continuity; sometimes they are part of the craft or the work itself. They cannot manufacture deserved importance where none exists. Compounding is a possible result of the whole relationship, not a reason to store every output or postpone system building until after value is already proven.

## The Kind Of Person This Honors

The people Yuzheng repeatedly returns to—Steve Jobs, Lionel Messi, Warren Buffett, Christopher Nolan, Geoffrey Hinton, Tsunekazu Nishioka, and others—worked in radically different media. They are not proof of a theory, and the brand does not borrow their fame as an endorsement.

What makes them emotionally relevant is a recognizable pattern: a distinctive standard, years of craft and persistence, resistance and revision, a body of work that entered the world, and recognition eventually earned through that work. Their work became difficult to separate from their names and continued to create value, meaning, or possibility after the first performance or release.

The aspiration includes success and recognition. It is not merely to be morally diligent or useful in obscurity. But fame is neither the definition nor a reliable verdict: popular work can be shallow, and important work can remain under-recognized. The standard is a work whose value, authorship, and continued life reinforce one another.

## Why Superlinear Exists

Superlinear is Yuzheng's defining work in progress and the institution he is building around this belief. It has two responsibilities at once:

1. to embody the standard in its own teaching, products, community, design, and operating choices;
2. to make it more possible for other people to pursue work of their own through understanding, practice, high-quality content, feedback, peers, and opportunities.

The institution cannot make anyone's defining work on their behalf, and joining it is not a guarantee of transformation. Its purpose is to improve the environment in which serious work is chosen, made, tested, and sustained. Superlinear Academy is the parent institution and master brand: its free community, AI Builders, Stay Superlinear membership, and enterprise programs each take responsibility for a different part of that environment, while books, content, and tools extend the same work; [ECOSYSTEM.md](ECOSYSTEM.md) owns the current relationship.

Yage is an important long-term teaching partner and technical authority whose research and engineering judgment make the educational work substantially deeper. Learners and members remain the protagonists. What they understand, decide, make, and become able to do offers especially important evidence of whether the institution is useful; some of their work may also earn reasons to remain.

## Working Principles That Remain Useful

The following are supporting lenses, not additional brand pillars:

- In consequential decisions with complex causality, inspect assumptions, incentives, constraints, and second-order effects instead of equating more information with better judgment.
- Make important views specific enough to be challenged, and allow evidence to change them. Debate, experiments, and community discussion are possible tools; none automatically produces truth.
- Prefer work that can improve the next move when the activity and conditions genuinely allow accumulation. Do not treat every meaningful act as an asset, every reusable artifact as compounding, or focus as universally superior to exploration and risk diversification.
- For work whose value depends on adoption, building includes helping the right people encounter, understand, use, and evaluate it. Narrative and distribution are interfaces to value, not substitutes for it.
- People are shaped by the standards, questions, information, and relationships around them. A good environment can widen possibility, but it does not own a person's growth and should not imply dependence.

## What Is Deliberately Not A Timeless Thesis

Several ideas remain useful in their proper contexts but no longer belong in the durable personal philosophy:

- `AI-native work` and `AI-native talent` are current category hypotheses about how work and professional capability may change when AI alters capability, cost, and constraints. They help translate this belief into the present technological moment; they are not a second defining idea, an age-based identity, or a timeless personality standard. [DATED_VIEWS.md](DATED_VIEWS.md) owns the current definition and its update conditions.
- `User → Builder → Architect` is not a universal human or career ladder. For people seeking more agency in some kinds of knowledge and software work, turning tool use into workflows, products, or assets they can own may be one valuable path.
- “AI moves scarcity upward” is too mechanical as a general law. AI is reducing some execution costs and changing complementary bottlenecks; the direction and magnitude depend on the task, demand, reliability, integration, and market structure.
- “Spend computation to conserve human cognition” is a conditional operating heuristic. Machine generation can also increase review, integration, security, and maintenance burdens.
- The capability / workflow / evaluation / ownership model is a useful enterprise diagnostic, not a universal law of organizational transformation.
- “A second Renaissance” is an evocative analogy, not an established forecast or a durable description of the community.

These claims can still guide content, courses, experiments, or enterprise work when their scope and date are explicit. See [DATED_VIEWS.md](DATED_VIEWS.md).
