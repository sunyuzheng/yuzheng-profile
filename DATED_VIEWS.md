# Dated Views And Working Hypotheses

This file keeps time-sensitive judgments out of the durable personal philosophy. It is an index and update discipline, not a hall of predictions or a claim that every view was correct.

For exact public wording, publication dates, links, and later outcomes, use [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md) and `facts.json`. Before quoting a judgment, return to the original source rather than reconstructing it from the summary below.

## Record Standard

A durable record of a dated view should contain:

1. the date and original context;
2. the exact claim or a clearly labeled paraphrase;
3. the scope and assumptions at the time;
4. the source link or archived evidence;
5. what happened next;
6. the current status: retained, narrowed, revised, falsified, or unresolved;
7. what new evidence would change the current view.

The purpose is intellectual continuity: make it possible to inspect how a judgment formed and changed. It is not to manufacture a mythology of permanent foresight.

## Current AI-Era Working Hypotheses

These are current hypotheses as of 2026-08-01. They are not timeless brand beliefs.

### AI is lowering some execution costs and changing complementary bottlenecks

In parts of digital and knowledge work, models can reduce the cost of drafting, coding, searching, analysis, and first versions. That can make problem selection, context, evaluation, reliability, integration, distribution, trust, or organizational adoption more consequential—but the bottleneck does not mechanically move “upward.” Lower costs can also expand demand, create new review burdens, or make an old bottleneck disappear.

`AI越会做题，人越要会出题` is a public shorthand for one implication: people still have to decide what is worth doing, for whom, what counts as useful, and who owns the consequences. It is not a claim that AI cannot suggest questions, help define a problem, or participate in evaluation.

**Update conditions:** task-level evidence on total lifecycle cost, quality, adoption, demand expansion, and where work actually stalls.

### Cheap machine iteration can buy information in bounded settings

In low-risk, isolated, and verifiable work, it can be rational to spend more computation on prototypes, comparisons, search, and disposable tools in order to reduce uncertainty. This is not permission to ignore privacy, security, maintenance, ownership, energy, or the human attention needed to review generated work.

**Update conditions:** evidence on review cost, incident risk, reliability, maintenance burden, and whether the iteration produced decision-relevant information.

### Durable enterprise adoption may require changes beyond tool exposure

For some teams, durable value from AI may depend on how capability connects to a real workflow, credible evaluation, clear ownership, and the organization's incentives and constraints. Which layer is actually binding is an empirical question. The capability / workflow / evaluation / ownership model is a current diagnostic frame, not a claim that every organization requires every layer to change or requires a large redesign.

**Update conditions:** observed adoption across team sizes, workflow types, risk levels, and operating models; evidence of which layer was actually limiting.

### Moving from use toward owned work can increase agency for some people

For people with the goal and capacity to build, turning occasional tool use into a workflow, product, body of work, or asset they can control may increase autonomy and leverage. This is not a universal `User → Builder → Architect` hierarchy, and it does not make experts, operators, employees, or users lesser roles.

**Update conditions:** ownership, maintenance cost, market demand, portability, risk, and whether the work genuinely expands the person's choices.
